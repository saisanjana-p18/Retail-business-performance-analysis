# Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data using MySQL to understand business performance, profitability, customer contributions, and sales trends.

## Dataset
- **Dataset:** Supermart Grocery Sales – Retail Analytics
- **Total Records:** 9,994
- **Unique Customers:** 50
- **Time Period:** 2015–2018

## Tools Used
- MySQL
- MySQL Workbench
- SQL

## Business Questions
- What are the overall sales and profit?
- Which product categories generate the highest profit?
- Which sub-categories have the lowest profit margins?
- How do discount levels relate to profit?
- Which regions contribute the most sales and profit?
- Who are the top 10 most profitable customers?
- Are there any loss-making transactions?
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- Which cities generate the highest sales?

## SQL Concepts Used
- Aggregate functions: `SUM()`, `AVG()`, `COUNT()`
- Grouping and sorting: `GROUP BY`, `ORDER BY`
- Filtering: `WHERE`
- Limiting results: `LIMIT`
- Date conversion and extraction: `STR_TO_DATE()`, `YEAR()`, `MONTH()`
- Null-handling: `COALESCE()`
- Calculated metrics and profit margin

## Key Findings
- Total sales were approximately 14.96 million.
- Total profit was approximately 3.75 million.
- Snacks generated the highest total profit among the categories.
- Snacks had the highest category-level profit margin.
- Oil & Masala had the lowest total profit and category-level profit margin.
- The dataset covers sales records from 2015 to 2018.

## Conclusion
This project uses SQL to explore retail business performance and identify differences in profitability across product categories, sub-categories, regions, customers, and time periods.
